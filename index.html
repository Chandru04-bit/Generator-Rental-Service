How Our Rental Process Works

From the initial capacity calculation to on-site commissioning and fuel maintenance, our 4-step workflow guarantees uninterrupted operations.

01

Power Sizing

Our engineers evaluate your peak kVA demand, motor starting loads, and fuel run-hours to specify the ideal unit.

02

Rapid Transport

Dedicated crane and flatbed fleet dispatches your generator, external fuel tank, and cables directly to site.

03

Setup & Commissioning

Certified technicians wire all connections, configure the ATS switch, and conduct a full load bank verification.

Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions regarding our generator fleet, delivery logistics, fuel management, and rental agreements.

Generator capacity is calculated based on total running watts (kW) plus the motor starting surge requirements (kVA). Equipment with large electric motors (such as cranes, air compressors, and chillers) typically requires 2.5 to 3 times their running power during startup. You can contact our technical engineering team with your site single-line diagram or load schedule, and we will calculate the exact kVA requirement.

Every PowerRent rental includes the fully serviced sound-attenuated generator, initial full fuel tank, delivery and positioning via hiab crane truck, certified power cabling up to 25 meters, automatic voltage regulation (AVR), 24/7 technical hotline access, and scheduled fluid and filter servicing every 250 runtime hours.

For emergency scenarios, our dedicated rapid response units can be mobilized from our nearest regional depot within 60 to 90 minutes. Our emergency dispatch hotline operates 24 hours a day, 365 days a year.
