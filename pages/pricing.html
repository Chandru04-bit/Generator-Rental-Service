Frequently Asked Pricing Questions

Everything you need to know about billing cycles, deposit refunds, fuel policies, and contract extensions.

A refundable security deposit is authorized on your payment card or corporate purchase order at the time of reservation. Once our logistics team conducts the post-rental inspection (confirming no physical damage, fuel level check, and cable returns), the deposit hold is released in full within 48 business hours.

All base rates include the certified generator set, sound-attenuated acoustic canopy, integral double-walled fuel tank, basic cable set (25m), cellular telemetry monitoring, and all scheduled routine maintenance (such as 250-hour oil/filter changes). Delivery, fuel consumption, and specialized electrical tie-ins are itemized separately for complete transparency.

All generators are dispatched with a 100% full fuel tank. You may refill the generator on-site prior to collection using clean, certified diesel or HVO fuel. Alternatively, PowerRent's fuel dispatch trucks can refuel the generator on site at standard local fuel index rates plus a nominal pumping fee.

Yes. Simply notify your dedicated account manager or submit an extension request directly through the customer dashboard at least 24 hours prior to scheduled collection. If your extension moves your rental into weekly or monthly thresholds, retroactive discounts are automatically applied to your account.
