General Information

Company & Service Overview

Learn about PowerRent's operational coverage, equipment standards, and electrical engineering capabilities.

Rates & Fees

Pricing, Discounts & Inclusions

Clear guidelines on daily, weekly, and monthly rates, volume tiers, and additional services.

Our automated booking engine automatically factors in rental duration thresholds: contracts from 7 to 29 days receive a 10% discount off cumulative day rates, while contracts of 30 days or longer receive an automatic 20% discount.

All quotes include the certified generator unit, weather-proof acoustic enclosure, integral base fuel tank, standard 25m power cable bundle, cellular telemetry monitoring, and all routine 250-hour preventative maintenance visits.

Our standard rental rates cover up to 12 running hours per calendar day (84 hours per week). Continuous 24/7 baseload packages with dedicated multi-shift service cycles are available at clearly specified supplemental hourly rates.
Reservations

Booking Schedules & Delivery

How far in advance to book, emergency call-outs, and extension flexibility.

For scheduled events and planned maintenance shutdowns, reserving 1 to 2 weeks in advance ensures your preferred capacity and cable configurations are set aside. However, our rapid response staging reserve allows immediate same-day dispatch within 2 hours.

You can extend your hire period at any time directly through your customer dashboard or by calling your dedicated dispatch coordinator with 24 hours advance notice. Retroactive volume discounts are applied as your duration crosses weekly or monthly tiers.

Bookings canceled more than 48 hours prior to scheduled delivery receive a 100% full refund. Cancellations made between 24 and 48 hours incur a nominal 10% administrative processing fee.
Invoicing & Accounts

Payment, Deposits & Corporate Credit

Supported payment gateways, corporate terms, and deposit return protocols.

We accept all major credit cards (Visa, MasterCard, American Express), ACH direct bank debit, and wire transfers. For verified corporate clients and governmental contractors, Net-30 purchase order terms are available upon credit approval.

Support & Fuel

Maintenance, Fuel & Telemetry

Routine servicing, fuel logistics, and emergency breakdown response protocols.

All routine servicing is performed directly on-site by certified PowerRent technicians. Every 250 operational hours, our mobile service van conducts full oil, lube, fuel filter replacements, and cooling system checks at zero additional cost to you.

Generators operate on ultra-low sulfur diesel (ULSD) or 100% paraffinic HVO biofuel. Units are delivered 100% fueled. For multi-day or continuous baseload contracts, our cellular telemetry automatically triggers our fuel bowsers to refill the unit whenever fuel drops below 30%.

Our generators transmit live telemetry 24/7. In the rare event of an engine warning or fault code, our central dispatch room is notified instantly and mobilizes our on-duty mobile service van within 30 to 60 minutes, with a replacement swap unit guaranteed if required.
