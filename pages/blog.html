Best Practices

Generator Rental Tips & Field Guidelines

Proven operational protocols compiled by our fleet technicians to prevent engine wet-stacking, voltage dips, and fuel contamination.

Account for Inrush Amps

Electric motors, pumps, and chillers require 3 to 6 times their running current upon initial start. Always size for peak startup demand.

Avoid Light Load Wet-Stacking

Running a diesel generator below 30% capacity causes unburned fuel buildup. Pair units with a resistive load bank or downsize your set.

Strategic Sound Placement

Direct generator exhaust louvers away from residential boundaries and use acoustic sound barriers to lower noise by an additional 6 to 10 dB(A).

Clean Fuel Protocols

Ensure fuel supplies are moisture-free and treated with biocide in sub-zero weather to eliminate paraffin waxing and filter clogging.
