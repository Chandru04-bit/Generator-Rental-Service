Company Introduction

Built on Engineering Rigor. Driven by Relentless Uptime.

Our Background

Founded in 2011, PowerRent was born out of a critical gap in industrial equipment rental: contractors and plant operators frequently suffered catastrophic downtime due to aging, poorly maintained generators, uncertified field connections, and sluggish emergency dispatch times. We resolved to transform temporary power hire from an uncertain expense into a reliable engineering partnership.

Who We Are

We are a dedicated collective of licensed electrical engineers, certified master generator technicians, and 24/7 rapid-response logistics coordinators. Operating modern staging depots strategically situated across major industrial corridors, we maintain a comprehensive rental fleet of over 350 silenced, emission-compliant generators ranging from 5 kVA portable units to 1,250 kVA multi-megawatt systems.

What We Do

PowerRent provides complete, turnkey power solutions: precise electrical load sizing, heavy transport and crane positioning, certified cable tie-ins, automatic transfer switch (ATS) synchronization, full resistive load-bank testing, on-site fuel replenishment management, and continuous live cellular telemetry monitoring.

Streamlined Workflow

Our 4-Step Generator Rental Process

How we deliver turnkey power from initial sizing to on-site commissioning and demobilization.

01
Understand Requirements

Our electrical engineers assess your site demands, calculating steady-state kW, motor starting inrush surge, voltage, frequency, phase balance, and site physical constraints.

02
Select the Right Generator

We configure the exact equipment combination: canopy acoustics, internal or auxiliary fuel tank capacity, distribution boards, step-up transformers, and automatic transfer switches.

03
Arrange Delivery & Setup

Dedicated transport delivers the generator on schedule. Certified technicians handle crane offloading, grounding rods, electrical cabling, phase rotation testing, and live commissioning.

04
Maintenance & Support

Continuous cellular telemetry monitoring, scheduled oil and filter servicing, automated fuel tanker replenishment, and 24/7 on-call technician standby ensure zero unexpected downtime.
